# Updating your XuVerse content

Open **Connect → Admin login**, then use the dashboard. Routine content changes do not need code edits. Keep a database and uploads backup before making major changes.

## Identity and page copy

In **Settings → Edit Settings**:

- **Site Title** is the site brand. **Display name** is your public name. **Homepage headline**, **Hero Description**, **Hero Kicker**, **Availability** and **Location** describe you without limiting your work to one discipline.
- **Navigation CTA Label** names the Connect navigation button. **Primary CTA** names the homepage Connect button; **Secondary CTA** names the button that opens the homepage work areas. These fields change labels, not destinations.
- **Resume headline** and **Resume summary** control the resume introduction. Experience, education and skills have separate dashboard editors.
- **Current Focus** and **Media Intro** edit their public sections. **Contact intro** introduces Connect; **CTA Title/Text** edit its email panel. **Footer Tagline/Text** edit the footer.
- **Contact Email** and the six social URL fields update your contact options. Use complete `https://` links; leave unused social links blank.
- **Meta Description** describes the homepage for search and sharing. Aim for a clear, accurate 140–155 characters; search engines may choose their own excerpt.

## Work, writing and media

**Projects:** write a short description, then add status, your role/scope, focus, evidence/results and the fuller case study. Optional repository and demo links should lead to real work. Lower display-order numbers appear first. Keep **Publish this project** unchecked until ready; drafts stay off public listings, project pages and the sitemap.

**Videos:** add the direct video URL and optional thumbnail. Keep **Publish this video** unchecked while preparing it. Use a specific video URL, not a search-results page. **Photos** and **Articles** become public when saved; finish reviewing them before submitting.

For article and case-study text, use a blank line between paragraphs, `## Heading`, `### Subheading`, and `- Bullet text`. Do not paste HTML. Review the public result after saving.

## Images and profile photo

Use **Upload Avatar** on the dashboard for your profile photo. Upload JPG or PNG images up to **2 MB**; images are optimized automatically. Project and media cards use consistent frames, while each uploaded image retains its proportions and stays fully visible. Empty space around portrait or wide images is intentional. Project detail images use a larger 4:3 frame; cards use 16:10. Replacing a project/media image replaces that record's old uploaded file, so keep your original files separately.

## Lengths, dates and the PDF

Keep headings short enough to scan. Project titles allow 200 characters and project status 100; article titles and most identity/social fields allow 255. Role/organization fields allow 100; institution/qualification 150. Settings reject text exceeding a field's database limit. Navigation and homepage button labels allow 100, location 150, and availability 180; concise labels work best.

Leave an end date/year blank for a current role or ongoing study. End dates cannot precede start dates. Education years must be 1901–2155.

**Download PDF** generates a fresh resume from saved details each time; there is no separate PDF to edit or upload. Longer content can add pages. After changing your resume, download it and check page breaks and spelling.

## Before publishing

Replace sample or outdated details with your own verified achievements. State your actual contribution, dates and evidence; omit unsupported numbers or claims. Check each destination link and open the site on a phone.

The direct email link stays available. The contact form appears only when the server has configured sender/recipient addresses; working delivery also requires the hosting mail setup. Domain, HTTPS, mail delivery and search indexing are covered in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md).
